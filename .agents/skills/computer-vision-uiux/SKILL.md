# 👁️ Computer Vision & UI/UX Audit Skill

## 1. Eye-Tracking Saliency Heatmaps
- Simulate human visual fixation in the first 2,500 milliseconds of page view.
- Calculate whether attention is captured by the Primary CTA (Buy Button) or dispersed by oversized rotating banners, logos, or distracting videos.

## 2. WCAG 2.1 Luminance & Color Blindness Matrix
- Calculate real contrast ratios between CTA background and text (minimum 4.5:1 for AA and 7.0:1 for AAA).
- Test visibility under bright sunlight reflection (critical for outdoor/fishing niches) and verify Protanopia/Deuteranopia safety.

## 3. Mobile Thumb Zone & Fitts's Law
- Divide mobile screen into Natural Reach (Easy), Stretch, and Hard zones.
- Flag primary buttons located outside the natural thumb arc on screens above 6 inches.
